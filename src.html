The House

Founded by a woman,
made by women.

Kumukh began in Istanbul in 2021 with one idea: a scent should feel like a memory you chose for yourself. Ela Kumuk founded the house at seventeen and still carries every part of it herself: the compositions, the packaging, the price, the suppliers and every customer.

Kumukh is women-founded and women-made. The perfumes are created by women, for anyone who wants to wear a scent with a point of view.

Two compositions have come out of the atelier so far, each made in small batches, and new ones are in development. The house grows one customer at a time.

    Questions

    Before you order

    How does ordering work?

    Add your bottles to the bag and tap “Place order via WhatsApp”. Your order opens as a ready-written WhatsApp message to the house, and every order is confirmed personally with payment and delivery details.

    Where do you ship?

    Can I send it as a gift?

    Yes. Tick “This is a gift” in your bag and write a message; it will be included with the bottle.

    Do you offer corporate pricing?

    Yes. Orders of ten bottles or more receive volume pricing. Request a quote with the number of bottles and the occasion.

    Can you create a fragrance for my brand?

    Yes, through private label. Tell us about your brand in the private label form and we will follow up to discuss direction, quantities and timing.

    Which one should I choose?

    Fruits du Ciel is bright and fruity over warm musk. Jardin d'Éden is a rose built around lychee and peony. If you are unsure, take the three-question finder.
